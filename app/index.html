        Honesty notes

        • Hidden flags. PlayStation hides 28 of the 55 trophies until they are earned. Which trophies are hidden cannot be verified from public sources, so the hidden flags in this guide are this entry's own reading of the list — story and secret trophies are marked hidden, open-world activity trophies are not. Treat them as a guide, not as PlayStation's own list.
        • Guides are not playtested. The game has just launched. No guide here comes from playing it: each one is built from the official description and its count, with the sources named beside it. A specific step with no source gets no credit from us — if a guide says where, it says where it came from.
        • Missables are a warning, not a promise. A trophy is flagged missable when its trigger is a specific mission or a one-time moment. Mission replay may or may not let you earn it later; this guide does not assume it does.
        • No online trophies. The official list holds no online trophies, so the Online filter is empty on purpose.
